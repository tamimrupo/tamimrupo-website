Why I run a web agency with AI

AI changed the work. It didn't change what clients pay for.

I've been building websites for more than eight years. The tools kept changing — WordPress, Elementor, Figma, and now AI. But the job stayed the same: understand the business and build something it can actually run on.

AI changes how fast we can build. I'll admit that made me nervous at first. But a fast first draft is not the same as a result a client pays good money for. The value didn't disappear — it moved to positioning, to the decisions you make for the client, and to everything they need after launch.

Clients never wanted just a website. They want more enquiries, more sales, and less time wasted on maintenance. That's how I run QDesk IT: AI agents for the build, judgement for the decisions, and real care after launch.
