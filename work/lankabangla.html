✳Case study · 2024

LankaBangla Investments

A WordPress site for an investment bank in Dhaka.

Client
LankaBangla Investments Limited
Role
WordPress development
Year
2024

Stack: WordPress, Elementor Pro, custom CSS · Live site ↗

The brief

LankaBangla Investments needed a site that could hold a lot of content: services, transactions, insights, awards, and team information. It had to feel credible to institutional clients and still be usable for someone visiting for the first time.

What I did

I built the site in WordPress with Elementor Pro. The work included reusable templates for the main content types, the responsive front end, and an editing setup that lets the team publish new pages without rebuilding layouts. I also worked on the less visible parts: image sizes, clean CSS, and navigation that keeps important pages visible.

  • Reusable templates for services, transactions, insights, and team pages.
  • Responsive layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
  • Editing structure the internal team can maintain.
  • Front-end performance work on images, assets, and CSS.

Result

The site is live and the team can add pages without a developer. The structure has held up as the content has grown.
