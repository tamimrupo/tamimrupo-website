The brief

EsferaSoft offers a wide range of services to an international audience. The site needed to organise that offer without turning into a long list of capabilities.

What I did

I worked with the design team to build reusable WordPress templates for services, industries, and case studies. I handled the responsive front end, the component styling, and the performance pass. The structure makes it clear where someone should go next.

  • Reusable templates for services and industries.
  • Responsive components aligned with the brand system.
  • An SEO-conscious content structure.
  • Performance and asset optimization.
  • Editing patterns the internal team can maintain.

Result

A maintainable corporate site that supports sales conversations and can grow with the service catalogue.
